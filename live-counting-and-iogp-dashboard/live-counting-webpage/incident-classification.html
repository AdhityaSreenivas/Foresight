Incident Narrative

During planned mechanical maintenance on isolation valve XV-4022, an unisolated high-pressure condensate bypass was struck by a torque wrench assembly, causing an immediate catastrophic flange gasket rupture. High-pressure hydrocarbon liquid released (estimated 18 bar) directly into the personnel walkway. Critical barrier failure identified: energy isolation (LOTO) verification was incomplete.
