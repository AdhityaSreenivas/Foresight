Known Synthetic Artifacts & Model Limitations

Mandatory Disclosure
  • Template Leakage: Repeated narrative templates in the synthetic dataset artificially elevate random-split validation metrics (e.g. PR-AUC near 1.0). Grouped or temporal evaluation would show lower performance.
  • Stylistic Artefacts: Artificial phrasing patterns (e.g. "the observation was made at") introduce synthetic shortcuts that may not translate to real-world industrial reporting.
  • Uncalibrated Relative Score: Model score reflects statistical ordering on synthetic distributions and must not be interpreted as physical injury probability.
  • SHAP Interpretation: SHAP represents model feature contribution on the XGBoost structured branch only. It does NOT represent root cause or physical causality.
