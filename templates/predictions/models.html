Controlled Promotion & Training Provenance Architecture:

Retraining dynamically gathers all currently eligible labeled incidents in PostgreSQL. Genuine human review decisions on synthetic incidents provide human-approved training targets; unreviewed synthetic records provide benchmark targets. Synthetic simulation reviews, unreviewed reports, and records with insufficient evidence are strictly excluded from supervised targets. Newly trained models are saved in READY (CANDIDATE) status and require explicit administrative safety gate activation before serving predictions.

⚠️ Human approval of synthetic incidents indicates human review of generated examples; it is not equivalent to validation against real-world OIL HSE records.
