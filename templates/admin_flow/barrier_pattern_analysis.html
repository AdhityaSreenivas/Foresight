Architectural Distinction:
A Barrier is the actual physical or operational protective measure described in the incident (e.g., Safety Harness, Machine Guard, Isolation Valve, Gas Detector). IOGP Life-Saving Rules represent the safety domain and are maintained as independent dimensions. Effective safeguards are recognized as protective successes and are strictly separated from barrier deficiencies.
