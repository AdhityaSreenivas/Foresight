Preflight Audit & Data Integrity Disclosure

Concern-pattern matches: NOT CURRENTLY COMPUTABLE (Omitted). A rigorous repository and database audit of all 561,351 incident records established that structured control condition fields (control_condition, control_failed_bypassed) are unpopulated (100% unknown/null). Deterministic keyword matching for terms such as "isolation" or "bypassed" cannot reliably separate verified controls or effective isolations from actual barrier failures without contextually calibrated negation and failure parsing.

To maintain complete analytical truthfulness, only directly computable metrics (Matched observations, PSIF-linked observations, and Affected sites) are presented in V1.
